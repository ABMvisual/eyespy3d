# Eye Spy 3D: riddles and hints for proofing

Each object has up to three hints, shown one at a time: a nudge, where to look, then its name.
Edit the wording here or tell Claude what to change. The game reads them from the CONTENT block at the top of `mashup_es3d.js`.

Lines marked **CHECK** need your eye: the nudges were written from the riddle wording alone, and a few locations were unclear in your notes.
